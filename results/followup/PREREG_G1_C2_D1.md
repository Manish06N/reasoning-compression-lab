(arXiv 2606.25519) report "token inflation" under GREEDY decoding, 100 questions per benchmark, one run, and say inflation is stronger on the subset BF16 answers correctly and weaker or negative on the subset BF16 gets wrong. G1 asks what the BF16-vs-BF16 configuration placebo looks like in their design. Lotfi et al. (2606, Meta) study aggressive (3-bit) quantization and "overthinking" errors; C2 and D1 connect to that.
